# Grill family and wayfinder

**What this is.** A reference that settles what `grilling`, `grill-me`, `grill-with-docs`, and `wayfinder` each are, when to reach for which against the thinking-and-planning neighbors (built-in plan mode, `/explore-plan`, `/brainstorm`, `superpowers:brainstorming`, `/prompt-optimize`, `/kickoff`), how to get the most out of each, where they genuinely overlap, and what to do with each in a retirement sweep. Written 2026-09-18 from the skill files as shipped in `mattpocock-skills` plugin 1.2.3. The four loose copies under `skills/` are byte-identical to the plugin's (checked with `diff`).

**Where the files live, and why this doc describes rather than edits them.** All four are untracked loose copies of skills the `mattpocock-skills` plugin already ships (`.gitignore` lines 58–60 and 81; policy in `THIRD-PARTY.md`). `~/.claude/skills` is a symlink to the main checkout's `skills/`, so the loose copies are what fires on `/grill-me`, `/grill-with-docs`, `/grilling`, and `/wayfinder`; the plugin's copies fire as `/mattpocock-skills:<name>`. None has a row in `docs/command-skill-reference.md` or a card in `docs/usage-playbook.md`, by that same policy. Editing a loose copy is unreviewable and is lost on the next `npx skills` reinstall.

## 1. What each one is

Start with the split: **one engine, two shims, one dependency the shims differ by, and one thing that is not a grill at all.**

**`grilling` — the engine** (1,987 B, model-invocable). A design-tree interview. Every decision branches into the decisions that hang off it; the *frontier* is every decision whose prerequisites are already settled. Each round it asks the whole frontier at once, numbered, each question with its own recommended answer, then waits. Your answers reshape the tree and it recomputes the frontier; a question that depends on another still open this round belongs to a later round. Facts are its job (it dispatches subagents to look things up and never asks you for anything it could find); decisions are yours. It ends when the frontier is empty and refuses to act until you confirm a shared understanding. It writes nothing: the output is the understanding in the transcript. It carries no `disable-model-invocation` flag, so "stress-test my thinking" or any "grill" phrasing can route to it automatically.

**`grill-me` — a shim** (157 B, hidden from the model). Its entire body is "Call the Skill tool with `grilling`." It exists to give you a slash name that reads as an imperative. It carries `disable-model-invocation: true`, so only you can start it, and only by typing `/grill-me`. Behaviorally it *is* `grilling`.

**`grill-with-docs` — a shim with a payload** (247 B, hidden from the model). "Call the Skill tool twice, for `grilling` and `domain-modeling`." A session under it is a grilling session that leaves a paper trail as it goes. This exact two-skill pairing is also what `wayfinder` invokes for every decision it resolves, so it is the family's canonical form, not an add-on.

**`domain-modeling` — the dependency the two shims differ by** (plugin skill, 3,331 B; not under review here). The active glossary-and-ADR discipline: it challenges your terms against `CONTEXT.md`, writes each resolved term into that file the moment it settles (creating the file lazily on the first term), stress-tests domain relationships with invented scenarios, checks your claims against the code, and offers an ADR under `docs/adr/` only when a decision is hard to reverse, surprising without context, and the result of a real trade-off. It is why `grill-with-docs` writes files mid-interview and `grill-me` never does.

**`wayfinder` — a different animal** (11,908 B, hidden from the model). For an effort too big for one agent session. It charts a **map**: one issue on the repo's tracker labelled `wayfinder:map`, holding a named destination, a decisions-so-far index, a "not yet specified" fog section, and an out-of-scope list. Under it hang **decision tickets**, child issues whose body is a question rather than a build step, each typed `research` (AFK), `prototype` (human in the loop), `grilling` (human in the loop, the default), or `task` (the one type that does rather than decides, and only to unblock a decision). Blocking uses the tracker's native dependency links so the frontier is visible in the tracker's own UI. Two modes: **chart** (grill to name the destination, grill breadth-first for the open decisions, create the map and the tickets you can state precisely, wire blocking, fire research subagents, stop) and **work** (load the map, claim one ticket by assigning it to yourself, resolve it, record the answer as a resolution comment, close it, graduate any fog the answer cleared, stop). Never more than one ticket per session, research excepted. It plans and does not build; the pull to build is its own signal that the map is done. It depends on a tracker doc that `/setup-matt-pocock-skills` writes (`docs/agents/issue-tracker.md`, section "Wayfinding operations"), defaulting to the local-markdown tracker at `.scratch/<slug>/map.md` plus `issues/`. Its "refer by name, never by bare id" rule is the same rule your global `CLAUDE.md` imposes on me.

## 2. When to reach for which

Keyed on the situation you are in. The last column is what each is the wrong tool for.

| You are in this situation | Reach for | Wrong tool for it |
|---|---|---|
| You have a plan, decision, or design **in hand** and want it interrogated before you commit; one sitting; you will make every call | `grilling`, by saying "grill me on X" or typing `/grilling` (`/grill-me` is the same thing) | `/explore-plan` (asks you to pick one of *its* approaches, not to decide a tree); `/brainstorm` (generates instead of interrogating); any unattended run (it blocks on your answers) |
| Same, but the repo has or should have `CONTEXT.md` and ADRs, and you want decisions recorded as you make them | `/grill-with-docs` | plain `grilling` (leaves nothing behind); a scratch directory (it will create `CONTEXT.md` there) |
| The effort spans several sessions, you want decisions made one at a time, possibly in parallel sessions, with a durable index of what is already decided | `/wayfinder` once to chart, then `/wayfinder <map>` per session | anything one session can hold (its own step 2 stops and says so); building anything (it plans only); a brand-new idea with no repo or tracker yet (`/kickoff`) |
| You know **what** to build in this codebase and want approaches ranked with the code read first, then the build in the same session | `/explore-plan` | design questions not grounded in this codebase; multi-session efforts |
| The what is already shared and you want a plan file to approve before any edit, with minimal ceremony | built-in plan mode | reaching a design concept (it produces a plan asset whether or not the concept is shared yet) |
| You have nothing yet and want candidate directions for this project, filtered by a critic gate | `/brainstorm <mode>` (yours: nine modes, ultracode fan-out) | anything with a decision already in hand |
| You are about to build a feature and want a design and a committed spec before the implementation plan | `superpowers:brainstorming` (the plugin's; a different thing from `/brainstorm`) | divergent idea generation, despite its name; a stress-test with no build coming |
| You have a rough prompt for a builder session and want it rewritten with a model and effort pick | `/prompt-optimize` | thinking through the work itself (advisory, one-shot; it explicitly hands interview-style prompt design to the Cowork `prompt-builder`) |
| A brand-new project idea that needs a brief, phased plan, and a repo | `/kickoff` | anything inside an existing project (its own edge case routes that to feature mode) |
| You need a design decided while you are **not** there | none of the grill family, and not a wayfinder `grilling` ticket (the skill says an agent answering its own questions has broken the contract). Only wayfinder `research` tickets and `/brainstorm`'s fan-out are AFK-safe. | |

## 3. How to get the most out of each

### `grilling`

- **A good invocation** brings something with a tree in it: a plan, an architecture, a spec draft, a decision with sub-decisions hanging off it. Say what the shared understanding must cover; grilling has no destination concept of its own (wayfinder adds one), so without that it will grill every branch. State hard constraints up front so round one's frontier is real. Answer by number ("Q1 A; Q2 take your rec; Q3 no, because…"). Every question you skip is a silent assumption, which is exactly what the skill exists to prevent.
- **What wastes it:** a bare idea with no structure (one root question, then it invents a tree; that is `/kickoff` or `/brainstorm` work); answering its fact questions yourself (tell it to look them up); letting it run to frontier-empty on branches you do not care about instead of saying "close that branch"; a plan that is already a task list with nothing left to decide.
- **Model and effort:** Fable, xhigh, synchronous. The recommended answer on every question is the whole value, and a design call per question is the Planner/Builder Protocol's definition of judgment-first work. Never async: it blocks on you by design.
- **Traps.** (1) It leaves no artifact. Follow it with `/grill-with-docs`, a plan, or `/handoff-session`, or the understanding evaporates with the transcript. (2) It appears twice in the model's skill listing, as `grilling` and as `mattpocock-skills:grilling`, identical today; if the loose copy is reinstalled at a different upstream version than the plugin's, the two diverge silently and auto-routing picks one of them. (3) Automatic routing reaches *this* skill only. The other three are hidden.

### `grill-me`

- Nothing to optimize; it is a name. The one trap is the flag: `disable-model-invocation: true` removes it from the model's listing, so "grill me" in prose routes to `grilling`, never to this. The name is the entire feature.
- **Usage:** 3 invocations in `~/.claude/history.jsonl`, all on 2026-08-12, none since the 2026-08-21 move to raw upstream copies.

### `grill-with-docs`

- **A good invocation** runs at the root of a repo that has or should have `CONTEXT.md` and `docs/adr/`, on a subject that changes the domain model: new concepts, a renamed term, a hard-to-reverse call. Domain-modeling creates the files lazily on the first resolved term, so an empty repo is fine. Branch first: it writes files mid-interview, and the git workflow's branch-before-changes rule applies before the first question.
- **What wastes it:** throwaway thinking in a scratch directory; a subject with no vocabulary in it (the second skill then has nothing to do and is dead weight in context); expecting an ADR per decision (it offers one only when all three of its criteria hold).
- **Model and effort:** as `grilling`. Fable, xhigh, synchronous.
- **Traps.** Two skills load at once, so two instruction sets compete for the same turns; settle where `CONTEXT.md` and the ADR directory live in round one or domain-modeling will ask. Hidden from the model, so only `/grill-with-docs` starts it. It is also the family's only entry you have reached for since the reinstall: 9 invocations from 2026-08-24 to 2026-09-18 across five repos.

### `wayfinder`

- **A good charting invocation** brings the loose idea to a repo whose tracker doc already exists (seven of your repos carry `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md`, this one included) and is ready to name the destination first, because the destination fixes scope for every later ticket. Expect the session to end with a map and zero decisions resolved; that is the contract.
- **A good working invocation** is `/wayfinder <map>`, optionally naming a ticket. Expect exactly one decision resolved and the fog updated. For throughput, run several sessions in parallel on unblocked tickets; the assign-to-claim mechanism exists for that.
- **What wastes it:** an effort with no fog (it stops at step 2 by design, and that is the right outcome); using it to build; resolving more than one ticket per session; running a `grilling` ticket unattended; charting without the tracker doc (it tells you to run `/setup-matt-pocock-skills`).
- **Model and effort:** chart with Fable, xhigh, synchronous (breadth-first design calls). Work a `grilling` or `prototype` ticket with Fable at high to xhigh, synchronous. `research` tickets are AFK by design and belong on Sonnet or Opus subagents. `task` tickets take whatever the task needs.
- **Traps.** Hidden from the model, so only `/wayfinder` starts it. Its map lives on the tracker, not in the transcript, and your global `CLAUDE.md` already exempts wayfinder from the `tickets.md` manifest for that reason. It has never run here: zero `/wayfinder` invocations in history and no `map.md` under any `~/Projects/*/.scratch/`. Its sharpest overlap (`to-tickets`, `implement-spec`, `orchestrate`) is excluded from this doc by your call.

## 4. Where they overlap

One row per pair that actually overlaps. "Redundant" means one could stand in for the other with nothing lost; "different shape" means they share a purpose but not a mechanism.

| Pair | What overlaps | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `grill-me` ↔ `grilling` | Everything. The shim is the engine plus a name. | **Redundant.** |
| `grill-with-docs` ↔ `grilling` + `domain-modeling` | Everything in content; the shim is a two-skill macro. | **Redundant in content, different in shape.** It is the only one-word entry to the pair, and the pair is what wayfinder canonizes. |
| `grilling` ↔ `wayfinder` | Wayfinder *contains* grilling: it calls it to name the destination, to map breadth-first, and for every `grilling`-typed ticket. | **Containment, not redundancy.** Grilling is one session, transcript-only, depth by frontier. Wayfinder is many sessions, tracker-persisted, one decision each, with `research`, `prototype`, and `task` types grilling lacks. Wayfinder without grilling is an empty shell; grilling without wayfinder is complete. |
| `grilling` ↔ plan mode | Both stop before code. | **Different terminal state.** Plan mode ends in a plan asset you approve; grilling ends in shared understanding and no asset. See the note below. |
| `grilling` ↔ `superpowers:brainstorming` | The closest neighbor by shape: both interview toward a design and hard-gate implementation behind your approval. | **Partial redundancy; real difference in cadence and artifact.** Superpowers asks one question per message, proposes 2–3 approaches, writes and commits a spec to `docs/superpowers/specs/`, and mandates `writing-plans` next. Grilling asks the whole frontier per round with a recommendation on each, writes nothing, mandates nothing. They compete for the same slot: design before build. |
| `grilling` ↔ `/explore-plan` | Both stop for you before edits, and both recommend. | **Different shape.** Explore-plan's single decision is picking among 2–3 whole approaches, codebase-grounded, and it builds afterward. Grilling decomposes into many small decisions and never builds. Sequential (grill, then explore-plan), not substitutes. |
| `grilling` ↔ `/brainstorm` | Only the stop before any write. | **No real overlap.** Divergent generation by blind agent fleets versus convergent interrogation of what you brought. The trap is the word: "brainstorm" in prose pulls `superpowers:brainstorming`; `/brainstorm` is your nine-mode fleet. |
| `grilling` ↔ `/prompt-optimize` | None in purpose. | **Sequential.** After a grill, prompt-optimize is how the shared understanding becomes a builder-session prompt. It explicitly declines to interview. |
| `grilling` ↔ `/kickoff` | Both interview. | **Different scope.** Kickoff is thirteen fixed dimensions, one question at a time, ending in a brief, a phased plan, and a repo, for new projects only. Grilling is an open tree, frontier rounds, no artifact, any subject. Kickoff wins for a new project; grilling wins for a decision inside an existing one. |
| `wayfinder` ↔ `/kickoff` | Both turn a loose idea into structure. | **Different phase.** Kickoff creates the project in one session; wayfinder charts a route inside an existing repo over many. A kickoff brief's open questions and riskiest assumption are roughly a wayfinder destination plus fog. Sequential at most. |
| `wayfinder` ↔ plan mode, `/explore-plan` | None. | **Different unit.** Wayfinder is larger than a session and produces decisions; these fit in a session and produce build steps. A finished map is the input they want. |
| `wayfinder` ↔ `superpowers:brainstorming` | Its "too large for one spec, decompose into sub-projects" step. | **Different unit.** Superpowers decomposes into build sub-projects; wayfinder into decision tickets. |
| `wayfinder` ↔ `/brainstorm` | None. | `/brainstorm` finds candidate destinations; wayfinder assumes one. |

### On the prior argument: grilling before plan mode

`docs/ideas/grilling-before-plan-mode.md` records Pocock's claim that plan mode is eager to create an asset and that grilling reaches a shared design concept first. It leaves open whether grilling belongs in the superpowers gate ahead of plan mode ("one gate, two gates, or routed by work type") and says adjudicating it against `docs/ideas/anti-plan-maxing.md` is part of the same decision. Three things this comparison adds:

1. **The claim holds on reading, and it orders three tools, not two.** Plan mode's terminal state is a plan you approve. `superpowers:brainstorming`'s terminal state is a committed spec plus a mandatory hand-off to `writing-plans`, which makes it *more* asset-eager than plan mode, not less. Grilling's terminal state is "do not act until the user confirms shared understanding," with nothing written. On the asset-eagerness axis: grilling, then plan mode, then superpowers:brainstorming. The superpowers flowchart already gates plan mode behind brainstorming, so adding grilling *to* that gate stacks two interviews. The real choice is grilling *or* superpowers:brainstorming for the design slot, decided by cadence (frontier rounds versus one question per message) and by whether you want the spec file.
2. **"By work type" is the right answer, keyed on the shape of the unknown.** Unknown is *what we are building* (a tree of decisions you hold): grill. Unknown is *how, in this codebase*: explore-plan or plan mode. Unknown is *what we should even do*: `/brainstorm`. Two gates in sequence, decisions first and a plan second, is what wayfinder already does at ticket granularity.
3. **Anti-plan-maxing is not in tension with grilling** once the design concept is separated from the plan asset. Uncle Bob's complaint is that plans fall apart when reality diverges; grilling produces the concept and no plan, which is what makes "fiddle until right" directed rather than random. The combined rule is: grill, then build directly when change is cheap; grill, then plan when it is not. That is input to the week-long trial the idea doc proposes as its first step, not a decision. Both ideas stay "not committed".
